Why I Like Formula One

I enjoy watching Formula one because of the on track action it provides. I also like the behind the scenes development work that goes into the cars which can either make them gain positions or fall back depending on how well the team did back at the factory.

Key Points

Formula one cars are complex in many ways. Their front and rear wings move called "Active Aero"

Power Unit

The power unit is the core of the car. Without the ICE (Internal Combustion Unit) the car would go nowhere. Each team must build their own power unit and compete with a limited number throughout the 24 race season.

Tires

While the tires are the same for all the competitors, the teams still have to get the best grip out of them with the aerodynamics that they design into their cars.

Tracks

Every track has it's own unique characture. Some teams have a slick car making them fast in a straight line. That also makes their car slower on tracks with slow speed corners.

Driver Pairings
